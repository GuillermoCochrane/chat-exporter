AI Chat Exporter

Privacy Policy

How your data is handled.

Privacy First

AI Chat Exporter is designed with a privacy-first philosophy.

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information, conversation history, browsing activity, analytics, or usage statistics.

Local Processing

All processing is performed locally inside your browser.

When you export a conversation, AI Chat Exporter reads the conversation currently open in ChatGPT and generates the selected Markdown or JSON file directly on your device.

No conversation content is sent to external servers.

Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions required for its functionality.

  • downloads — Saves exported files to your device.
  • storage — Stores user preferences such as language, export format, compact mode, and export options.
  • Host permission (chatgpt.com) — Reads the currently open ChatGPT conversation only to generate the requested export.

Third-party Services

AI Chat Exporter does not use external services for processing or tracking.

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • Cloud storage
  • External APIs
  • Remote servers
