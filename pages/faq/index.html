Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI Chat Exporter.

General

What AI Chat Exporter is and how to get started.

What is AI Chat Exporter?

AI Chat Exporter is an open-source browser extension and CLI tool that lets you export your ChatGPT conversations as clean Markdown or JSON files, entirely on your device.

Which AI platforms are supported?

Currently, ChatGPT is fully supported. DeepSeek, Gemini, Claude, and others are on the roadmap.

Is it free?

Yes. AI Chat Exporter is free and open source under the MIT License. No hidden costs, no premium features.

Do I need an account?

No. The extension works with your existing ChatGPT session. No registration required.

Can I use it without the extension?

Yes. AI Chat Exporter includes a standalone CLI that converts exported ChatGPT JSON files into Markdown from the terminal.

Where can I find the source code?

The complete source code is available on GitHub under the MIT License. Contributions are welcome.

Privacy & Security

How your data is handled.

Are my conversations uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your conversations never leave your computer.

Does the extension collect any data?

No. AI Chat Exporter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, analytics, or usage data.

Does it use analytics or tracking?

No. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking scripts, and no third-party services of any kind.

For complete details, read the full Privacy Policy.

Export Features

What you can export and how to customize it.

What formats can I export?

Markdown and JSON. Markdown is optimized for documentation and note-taking; JSON preserves the original conversation structure.

What is Compact Mode?

Compact Mode reduces unnecessary spacing and removes quote formatting, producing smaller and cleaner Markdown files. Ideal for long conversations or documentation.

Can I export only user messages?

Yes. The role filter lets you export only user messages, only assistant messages, or the entire conversation.

Can I export only assistant messages?

Yes. Simply select "Assistant only" in the role filter before exporting.

Can I use exported Markdown in my documentation?

Absolutely. The generated Markdown is clean, readable, and ready for tools like Obsidian, Notion, or GitHub.

Permissions

Why the extension requests each permission.

Why does the extension need "downloads"?

To save the exported Markdown or JSON files directly to your device.

Why does it need "storage"?

To remember your preferences, such as language, export format, and filter options, across sessions.

Why does it need access to chatgpt.com?

The extension reads the currently open ChatGPT conversation to generate the export. It does not access any other websites or data.

Troubleshooting

Common issues and how to solve them.

Why can't I export a conversation?

Make sure you have a conversation open in ChatGPT and that the page has fully loaded. If you just navigated to the conversation, try refreshing the page first.

Why is my export empty or incomplete?

AI Chat Exporter captures the conversation as it was when the page loaded. Messages sent or received after that moment may not be included. For best results, reload the conversation before exporting.

The extension icon doesn't respond. What can I do?

Try refreshing the ChatGPT page. If the issue persists, remove and reinstall the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
