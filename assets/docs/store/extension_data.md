# AI Chat Exporter

**Own your conversations. Not just your prompts.**

AI Chat Exporter lets you save your ChatGPT conversations in open, portable formats that you truly own.

Export complete conversations as clean Markdown or JSON files, ready for documentation, research, backups, knowledge bases, or long-term archiving.

Everything happens locally in your browser. Your conversations never leave your computer.

---

## Why AI Chat Exporter?

AI conversations often contain valuable knowledge, but they usually remain locked inside a single platform.

AI Chat Exporter was created to solve that problem.

Simply open a ChatGPT conversation, click the extension icon, choose your preferred export options, and save your conversation in seconds.

---

## Features

* Export conversations as **Markdown** or **JSON**.
* Clean, readable Markdown optimized for documentation.
* Compact mode for smaller, cleaner exports.
* Filter exported messages by role:

  * User
  * Assistant
  * Complete conversation
* Quick export directly from ChatGPT.
* Open, portable file formats.

---

## Privacy First

Your conversations belong to you.

AI Chat Exporter:

* Processes everything locally inside your browser.
* Never uploads your conversations.
* Never sends data to external servers.

No accounts. No cloud processing. No hidden data collection.

# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 2026

AI Chat Exporter is designed with a privacy-first philosophy.

## Data Collection

AI Chat Exporter does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing activity, conversations, or usage data.

## Local Processing

All processing is performed locally on your device.

When you export a conversation, the extension reads the conversation currently open in ChatGPT and generates the selected output format (Markdown or JSON) entirely within your browser.

No conversation data is sent to external servers.

## Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions required for its functionality:

* **downloads** – to save exported files to your device.
* **storage** – to store user preferences such as export format or options.

These permissions are never used to collect analytics or personal information.

## Third-Party Services

AI Chat Exporter does not use:

* analytics services,
* advertising networks,
* tracking technologies,
* external APIs,
* cloud storage,
* remote servers.

## Open Source

The source code is publicly available at:

https://github.com/GuillermoCochrane/chat-exporter

Anyone can inspect, audit, or contribute to the project.

## Changes

If this privacy policy changes in future releases, the updated version will be published together with the project documentation.

## Contact

For questions or issues regarding this privacy policy, please use the GitHub repository:

https://github.com/GuillermoCochrane/chat-exporter/issues
