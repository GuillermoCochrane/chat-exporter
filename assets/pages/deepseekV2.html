    Why AI Chat Exporter?

    AI conversations often contain valuable knowledge, but they usually remain locked inside a single platform. AI Chat Exporter was created to solve that problem.

    Simply open a ChatGPT conversation, click the extension icon, choose your export options, and save your conversation in seconds. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.
